Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

The developer of Tumble ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what the Tumble iOS app ("the app") stores, where it is stored, and how it is used.

In short — Tumble has no accounts and no developer-operated servers. Your to-dos are stored only on your device and, if you are signed in, in your own iCloud. We cannot see them. Payments are handled by Apple, and we never receive your payment details. The app has no ads, analytics, or tracking.

1. Information Stored

The app does not collect information that identifies you, such as your name, email, phone number, or location. To provide its features, the app stores the following on your device:

To-dosText, category, date, repeat setting, completion state and time, creation time
SettingsCategory names and order, app language (Korean/English), last selected category
Widget dataPosition, rotation, color, and text of blocks used to draw the box on the Home Screen widget
PurchasesFree trial start date and whether you bought the lifetime unlock (Apple-signed purchase records checked on your device)

2. How It Is Used

  • Stacking to-do blocks, the to-do list, completed to-dos, and editing
  • Creating the next occurrence of repeating to-dos and marking overdue items
  • Restoring your settings when you reopen the app
  • Showing a mini map of your box on the Home Screen widget
  • Calculating the free trial period and verifying or restoring your lifetime unlock
  • Syncing to-dos across devices signed in to the same Apple Account, when iCloud is available

3. Where It Is Stored and For How Long

  • Device: To-dos and settings are stored on your device. Widget data is stored in an on-device shared container (App Group) that only the app and its widget can access.
  • iCloud: If your device is signed in to iCloud, to-dos are stored and synced in Apple CloudKit's private database in your own iCloud account. This uses your iCloud storage, and we cannot access it. App settings such as category names and language are not synced.
  • Information is kept until you delete it or delete the app.

4. Deleting Information and Your Rights

You can view, edit, and delete all of your information yourself.

  • Delete a to-do: long-press a block → Discard; swipe to delete a completed to-do in the Completed list
  • Delete all completed to-dos: To-do list › Completed › Clear all
  • Delete everything on the device: delete the app
  • Delete data in iCloud: iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage, then delete Tumble's data

To-dos deleted in the app are also deleted on your other synced devices. Deleted information cannot be recovered.

5. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell or share your information, and nothing is sent to a developer server. iCloud sync is provided by Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

The free trial and lifetime unlock are processed by the Apple App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple Account, or card or bank details; App Store Connect only shows us anonymous totals such as sales by country.

6. Device Features and Permissions

  • Motion sensors (accelerometer, tilt): Used in shake mode to move blocks as you tilt the device, and to turn on shake mode when you shake it. Sensor values are used only in the moment and are never stored or sent.
  • Keyboard input: Date words such as "tomorrow" or "every Monday" are interpreted on your device only.
  • Haptics: Used for feedback when you pick up and drop blocks.
  • The app does not request access to location, photos, contacts, camera, microphone, or notifications.

7. Ads, Analytics, and Tracking

The app contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If you choose to share analytics with Apple, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash and usage statistics.

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We recommend not writing sensitive information such as ID numbers, passwords, or health details in your to-dos.
