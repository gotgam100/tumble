FAQ

How does the free trial work?

Everything is available for 7 days from the day you first download the app, with no sign-up. See the days left on the "Free trial" badge at the top of the box or in Settings › Tumble full version. You are never charged automatically when it ends.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your to-dos stay, and you can still view, complete, and delete them. To add new ones, buy the one-time lifetime unlock (₩1,100 in Korea). It's not a subscription.

It's locked on another device or after reinstalling.

Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account, then tap Settings › Tumble full version › Restore.

Can my family use it?

With Family Sharing on, family members can use the lifetime unlock too. They just tap Restore on their own devices.

I'd like a refund.

Payments are handled by Apple, so please request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds directly.

How do I add a to-do?

Type in the field at the bottom and press return. A block drops in from the top. You can pick a category and date on the left of the field.

Can I type dates in words?

Write things like "dentist tomorrow", "report Friday", "birthday 10/20", or "workout every Monday" and the date and repeat are set automatically. The date words are removed from the to-do text.

How do I complete a to-do?

Tap a block once, or grab it and flick it up off the screen. You can also tap the circle in the to-do list. Completed to-dos stay in To-do list › Completed.

I completed or discarded something by mistake.

Tap "Undo" right away. Completed to-dos can also be put back in the box by tapping the green check in the Completed list.

How do I edit or delete a to-do?

Long-press a block to open the editor, where you can change the text, category, date, or repeat, or discard it.

What is shake mode?

Tap the shake button or shake your iPhone. Blocks bounce once, then tumble in the direction you tilt. It turns off after 5 seconds without movement or when you tap the button again.

iCloud sync isn't working.

Check that your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, that iCloud Drive is on in Settings › [your name] › iCloud, and that you have iCloud storage left. "iCloud sync" in the app's settings should show On. Changes can take a few minutes to appear on other devices.

The widget doesn't update right away.

The widget redraws when blocks settle in the app or when you leave the app, and iOS may delay updates. Try opening and closing the app.

Can I rename categories?

Yes, in Settings › Categories you can change names and order. Each category's block shape and color are fixed.

How do I change the app language?

Choose Korean or English in Settings › Language. It changes immediately.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device and in your own iCloud. We cannot see it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
